---
name: Wide Present composition
description: Vertical alignment rule for live Present boards across tall and constrained classroom surfaces
---

Tall, wide live Present boards should center the authored task/prompt stack and the actual visual/timer rail children within the main composition. Short landscape, narrow, editor-preview, and fixed export surfaces should keep their compact or established top-aligned behavior.

**Why:** The wide 16:9 surface otherwise leaves a large unexplained lower void while the student content and rail cluster at the top. Centering only the student composition preserves the footer row, divider, content hierarchy, and intrinsic visual framing without adding decoration or persisted layout state.

**How to apply:** Scope the balance to live surfaces with enough width and height; measure authored children rather than the full-height content grid item in browser coverage; assert relative centers and whitespace, not exact screenshots.
